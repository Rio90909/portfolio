About

The editor behind the cuts

I'm Iman Arif, a video editor who believes every second of a video has to earn its place. My style is minimal and modern — clean cuts, honest pacing, and color that feels like the future, not a filter.


Whether it's a 30-second reel that has to stop the scroll, or a full YouTube video that has to hold attention to the last frame — I edit for the person watching, not the person filming.
